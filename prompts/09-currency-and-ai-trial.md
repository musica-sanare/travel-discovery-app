# Prompt 09 — Source-currency costs, dated operations checks, and an AI trial plan

Production has 18 published destinations with sourced `JPY` cost estimates for six of them, best months for nine, and best-season text for twelve (`content/initial-v2.json`, ADR-028, "Phase 8 verification log"). Budgets in `USD` or `EUR` cannot be compared with any destination, because estimates exist only in `JPY` and the app does not convert currencies (ADR-021). This phase adds estimates in the currency the sources publish, finishes the Workers Logs checks due before 2026-12-01, and prepares the owner's decision on a preview AI trial. The first response is investigation and a plan only: no implementation, configuration change, or deployment.

Do not run remote Cloudflare commands, deploy, change account settings, push, commit, add dependencies, download browsers, enable AI, or enable any paid plan or feature without the owner's explicit approval for that action. Dashboard and Access changes are the owner's steps; describe them, do not perform them. The account must stay on Workers Free: the only accepted charge is the owner's existing domain registration.

`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md` (ADR-008, ADR-021, ADR-026 "Readiness for enabling AI" and "Open decisions", ADR-028 including "Update mode", "Phase 8 verification log", and "Open questions" item 6), `docs/DIAGNOSTIC_ALGORITHM.md` section 5, `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` sections 7 and 9, `docs/OPERATIONS.md` sections 3, 7, 8, and 10, `content/initial-v2.json`, and `test/node/content-bundle.test.ts`.

## Tasks
1. **Workers Logs before 2026-12-01.** The owner reads the daily log volume of the four Workers in the dashboard. In the last week of November 2026, re-read https://developers.cloudflare.com/observability/pricing/ and https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/observability/logs/workers-logs/, compare the volume with the Free allowance of 0.5 GB per day, and update `docs/OPERATIONS.md` section 7, `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` section 9, and the open item in `docs/OPERATIONS.md` section 9 with the dates checked. If the volume approaches the limit, propose a `head_sampling_rate` change instead of making it.
2. **Estimates in the source currency.** For destinations whose verified source publishes figures in `USD` or `EUR` (Phase 8: Taipei, Buenos Aires, and Honolulu in `USD`; Rome and Sicily in `EUR`), add a second estimate in that currency with the same source, scope, period, range rule, and date checked, without any conversion. Amounts are whole currency units, so state the rounding (to the nearest unit) in the basis for `ja`, `en`, and `es`. Sydney's source publishes `AUD`, which the app does not support: leave it `JPY` only. Before adding anything, re-read each source: replace Hawaii's preliminary 2025 figure if the final one is published, and re-check the Buenos Aires tourism site for a recommended season. Put the result in a new self-contained bundle in `content/` with tests passing, give the owner a rendered review copy (all three locales, every source linked), and import on preview, then production, with the update mode and Time Travel bookmarks before and after (`docs/OPERATIONS.md` section 8).
3. **Remaining empty costs (time-boxed).** Search again only where Phase 8 found a likely candidate (for example a per-day figure for Seoul or an official per-day figure for Bangkok). Apply the Phase 8 rules ("Phase 8 verification log", owner decisions); leave a destination empty when nothing qualifies, and record what was checked.
4. **Preview AI trial plan.** Production content now exists, which was the last unmet item in "Readiness for enabling AI". Write the plan for the trial in `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` 7.1: the expected Neuron use and the Workers Free daily limit (whether preview and production share it is 要確認; plan as if they do), how to confirm that `AI_CLIENT_RATE_LIMITER` answers 429, which `ai_explanation` lines to check, what the owner reviews in the notes in `ja`, `en`, and `es`, the stop procedure, and what would block a later production decision. Do not enable AI: the trial and any production use are separate owner decisions.

Deferred, not part of this phase: images (privacy and license decision first), checking that an address outside the Access policy is refused (needs a second Cloudflare account), the slow first TLS use of Node on the owner's Mac, and a dated exchange-rate table for comparing budgets across currencies ("Open questions" item 6).
