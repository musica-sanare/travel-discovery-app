# Prompt 10 — Preview AI trial, Observability pricing follow-up, and a rollback check

Phase 9 added source-currency estimates, imported them on both environments, documented the preview AI trial (`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` 7.1), and recorded the Workers Logs volume before Cloudflare Observability pricing starts on 2026-12-01 ("Phase 9 verification log"). This phase checks the account after that pricing change, runs the preview AI trial if the owner decides to, closes the open rollback question, and re-checks one empty content item. Start only after Phase 9's acceptance is met. The first response is investigation and a plan only: no implementation, configuration change, or deployment.

Do not run remote Cloudflare commands, deploy, roll back, change account settings, push, commit, add dependencies, download browsers, enable AI, or enable any paid plan or feature without the owner's explicit approval for that action. Dashboard and Access changes are the owner's steps; describe them, do not perform them. The account must stay on Workers Free: the only accepted charge is the owner's existing domain registration. After deploying an admin Worker, immediately check without signing in that it redirects to Access; on a 200, redeploy with `workers_dev: false` and report.

## Read first
`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md` ("Phase 9 verification log", ADR-015, ADR-026 "Stage 5 preparation decisions", "Open decisions", and "Readiness for enabling AI", ADR-028), `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` sections 5, 6, 7, 7.1, 8, and 9, `docs/OPERATIONS.md` sections 3, 4.4, 7, 9, and 10, and `scripts/check-deployment.mjs`.

## Tasks
1. **Observability pricing after 2026-12-01.** In the first week of December 2026, re-read https://developers.cloudflare.com/observability/pricing/ and https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/observability/logs/workers-logs/. The owner reads, in the dashboard, the account's log ingestion (Billable Usage, if it is shown) or the Observability event counts, and the Billing page. Record that ingestion stays within the Free allowance and that nothing new is charged, in `docs/OPERATIONS.md` section 7 and `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` section 9, with the dates. Do the monthly checks of `docs/OPERATIONS.md` section 3 for December at the same time. If the volume approaches the limit, propose a `head_sampling_rate` change instead of making it.
2. **Preview AI trial (owner decision first).** Ask the owner whether to run the trial. If they decline, record that and skip this task. If they approve, follow `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` 7.1 exactly: each configuration commit and each of the two preview deployments needs its own approval; at most 10 model calls; stop at once on any stop condition. Record the result in `docs/DECISIONS.md` as 7.1 lists, without copying the notes into the repository, and update ADR-026 "Readiness for enabling AI" and the open items in `docs/OPERATIONS.md` section 9. AI is disabled again at the end; any production use is a separate owner decision, not part of this phase.
3. **Rollback of static assets (time-boxed).** `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` section 8 leaves open whether a Worker rollback also restores its static assets (要確認). Propose a check on the public preview Worker only: which earlier version to roll back to, how to tell the served client bundle apart (for example its asset file name), how to return to the current version, and what `scripts/check-deployment.mjs` must show afterwards. Run it only with the owner's approval for each rollback, record the result in section 8, and leave production untouched.
4. **Empty content re-check (time-boxed).** Re-check https://turismo.buenosaires.gob.ar/ for a recommended season. If the site is available and an official recommendation meets the Phase 8 rules ("Phase 8 verification log", owner decisions), prepare a self-contained bundle with that change only, give the owner a rendered review copy, and import it with the update mode and Time Travel bookmarks as in Phase 9. Otherwise record the date checked and change nothing.

Deferred, not part of this phase: production AI, images (privacy and license decision first), a dated exchange-rate table ("Open questions" item 6), checking that an address outside the Access policy is refused (needs a second Cloudflare account), new searches for the other empty costs, and the pre-public-release checklist (`docs/PUBLIC_RELEASE.md`).

## Acceptance
- The pricing pages are re-read after 2026-12-01, and the account's log ingestion and Billing page are recorded with dates; the account stays on Workers Free with no new charge.
- Either the owner's decision not to run the AI trial is recorded, or the trial is recorded as `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` 7.1 requires (including whether `AI_CLIENT_RATE_LIMITER` answered 429) and AI is disabled again in every environment.
- The rollback question in `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` section 8 is answered from an approved preview check, or left as 要確認 with the reason.
- The Buenos Aires season check is dated; any content change was reviewed by the owner and imported with bookmarks recorded.
- `npm run check` and `npm run test:e2e` pass after any code or content change.
